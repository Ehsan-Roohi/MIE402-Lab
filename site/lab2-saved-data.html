Use the data you already saved

If the MATLAB sine fit is nearly flat or misses the measured pendulum angles, you do not need to repeat the physical experiment. Use the saved ExpData MAT file in the supplied Colab notebook, inspect the output, and explain any missing tracking data in your report.

Start here: Open the Lab 2 saved-data notebook in Google Colab. You can also download the notebook. No programming is assigned: run the provided cells from top to bottom.

What you need

Upload the .mat file that Pendulum Motion Analyzer saved, or a .zip containing one such MAT file. The MP4 alone is not an input to this notebook. Keep your raw files; the notebook does not overwrite them.

What the notebook does

  1. Reads time and tracked bob coordinates from ExpData.
  2. Converts each valid position to an angle measured from the vertically downward direction.
  3. Fits a sinusoid whose amplitude may decrease over time and displays the approximate frequency and period.
  4. Counts valid frames and the longest period without tracked positions.
  5. Creates Lab2_report_figure.png and a PDF copy, then packages them with a results text file for download.

What to put in the Lab 2 portion of your report

Insert the generated figure. Its dots are measured angles; its dashed line is a model. The dashed portion over a gap is not a newly measured trajectory. Report the fitted frequency and period with units, compare the period with the theoretical prediction, and state the tracked-frame count or percentage and longest gap. Describe whether the model follows the observed dots. If tracking is sparse, call the frequency an approximate estimate and discuss that limitation. Do not claim the fitted decay coefficient separately measures air drag; pivot friction and tracking error may also contribute.

Suggested caption to adapt: “Measured pendulum angle (dots) and damped-sine model (dashed line). The approximate fitted frequency is [f] Hz, corresponding to [T] s. [n] of [N] frames have valid tracked positions, with a longest gap of [gap] s. The dashed curve across gaps is a model prediction, not measured data.” Replace every bracketed item with your own output.

If you still have the original video: you may re-run the analyzer on that existing video to recover more positions; no new laboratory recording is required. If only the saved MAT file is available, analyze its valid points and state the limitation. If the notebook reports fewer than 30 valid positions or the plotted fit clearly fails to follow the observed points, contact the teaching team with your original files instead of inventing values.
