From the apparatus to the measurements

The encoder wheel reports cart position from wheel rotation, provided it does not slip; Vernier Graphical Analysis connects to the correct cart serial number, starts collection before release and exports position-versus-time CSV files. The two pre-tensioned springs oppose displacement with incremental forces −k1x and −k2x; hence equivalent stiffness is their sum. The magnetic brake uses eddy currents to remove energy. Its velocity-proportional model is an approximation, and baseline rolling/air damping is not zero.

Dividing mẍ+cẋ+keqx=0 by mass yields the standard form ẍ+2ζωnẋ+ωn2x=0. Here ωn=√(keq/m) [rad/s] is undamped natural angular frequency, ζ=c/(2mωn) is dimensionless damping ratio, α=ζωn [1/s] is envelope-decay rate, and ωd=√(ωn²−α²) [rad/s] is damped angular frequency. For ζ between 0 and 1, the characteristic roots are −α±iωd; their real and imaginary parts produce the exponentially decaying sinusoid.

Measure time spacing between equal-sign peaks for Td, use logarithmic decrement δ=ln(pi/pi+N)/N=αTd or a log-peak straight-line fit to estimate α, then obtain ζ=α/√(ωd²+α²) and c=2mα. FFT peak frequency is a consistency check near ωd/(2π), not an independent estimate of c. See the full Lab 4 workflow for spring calibration, seven cases, unit checks, and residual analysis.

What students should understand before Lab 4

  1. The total moving mass includes the cart and every attached moving part.
  2. For a cart between two springs, the equivalent stiffness is the sum of left and right spring stiffnesses.
  3. Peak spacing gives damped frequency; the reduction of same-sign peaks gives damping.
  4. The FFT peak checks frequency near the damped natural frequency, but the FFT alone does not determine damping.
  5. Adding magnetic damper hardware can change both damping and moving mass.

Core model

md²x/dt² + cdx/dt + keqx = 0. The lecture defines every variable and unit before using it, derives the underdamped response, and connects each fitted quantity to a visible feature of the measured record.
