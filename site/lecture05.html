What students should understand before Lab 4

  1. The total moving mass includes the cart and every attached moving part.
  2. For a cart between two springs, the equivalent stiffness is the sum of left and right spring stiffnesses.
  3. Peak spacing gives damped frequency; the reduction of same-sign peaks gives damping.
  4. The FFT peak checks frequency near the damped natural frequency, but the FFT alone does not determine damping.
  5. Adding magnetic damper hardware can change both damping and moving mass.

Core model

md²x/dt² + cdx/dt + keqx = 0. The lecture defines every variable and unit before using it, derives the underdamped response, and connects each fitted quantity to a visible feature of the measured record.
