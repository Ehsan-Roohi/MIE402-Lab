Why engineers use a ring-down test

A free response after a bump, impact, or release is a compact system-identification test. Engineers use its oscillation frequency to diagnose the mass-stiffness combination and its decay rate to evaluate damping and settling. The same idea appears in vehicle suspensions, machine mounts, instruments, packaging, structures, and aerospace hardware.
